## Blog 11b – Reflecting on AT3

My AT3 project is progressing slowly but steadily. The idea is to visualise the live scores of The Summer Games, a weekly competition run by my friends in Canberra. Using p5.js, I’m creating a generative aura that shifts colours and behaviour depending on the game results. Each team (blue, green, pink, red) contributes to the animation, and the more goals that are scored, the more chaotic and organic it becomes.

### Communities of Practice

The Summer Games is more than just a group of friends playing sports, it's a group of high school friends that have stayed in touch for the past 6 or 7 years. They've expanded on their own group, and brought together partners, girlfriends, and other likeminded people in order to create an expanded community. I've met some of my best friends through the community. We all meet up on Sundays for the games every summer, every few weekends for birthdays or other celebrations, and the boys also meet up for pub night on Wednesdays. It's a tight nit group of friends with various smaller communities within it.

Our domain is informal sport, but it’s also social design. It lives across spreadsheets, Instagram posts, graphics, etc. The repertoire includes stats, team colours, chants, memes and inside jokes. I’m building on all of that by translating it into generative art.

The values of this community are pretty clear: fun, playfulness, rivalry, and (most importantly) friendship. That guides my decisions in the project. The visuals need to be expressive and energetic, not sleek or corporate.

### Mycelial Growth

Thinking through mycelial creativity has been helpful. My idea has gone through a few transformations already, from a literal stats dashboard to an abstract aura. I’ve been letting it grow in unexpected directions, following tangents and ideas that come up during the process. This growth hasn’t been linear, jumping between sound, animation, interaction and data. I’m don't build layer by layer, but instead letting things spread and connect where they feel right. There’s also an accumulative quality to it. Every version I make feels like it adds something, even if I end up scrapping it. I’m gathering visual behaviours and design choices.

### Fruiting Body

I think the fruiting body of this project is going to be the final interactive version that others can interact with in one way or another (scoring or watching). I’d like to make it so that when someone opens the page and chooses two teams, the aura instantly reflects their colours and score count. Even if the score isn’t live, it will visualise what has happened in that past game.
