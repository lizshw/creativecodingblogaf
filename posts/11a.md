## The Plan (Project Breakdown)

### Community of Practice: The Summer Games

- Domain: Informal, DIY sports competitions — with a creative and social twist.
- Repertoire: Instagram updates, live scoring, colour-coded teams, memes, hand-made graphics, and a shared love of chaotic competition.
- Values: Friendship, humour, weekly tradition, low-stakes joy, and showing up. It's a competition, but mostly we just love the ritual of it all.

### Code Explanation (p5.js)

The core idea is to create a generative "aura" that visually responds to the number of goals scored. The colours of the aura shift based on the scoring ratio of the two teams currently playing. More goals = more movement and chaos. The colour leans towards whichever team is scoring more.

- Uses `noise()` to generate an organic shape
- `map()` is used to shift hues based on live score input
- Future plans include WebSocket input for live updates, and maybe audio feedback too

### Practical Plan

- User chooses which two teams are playing
- As goals are entered, the aura shifts dynamically
- Each team’s colour (blue, green, pink, red) blends in based on their scoring dominance
- May later add a "final score" animation where the winning colour blooms across the screen
